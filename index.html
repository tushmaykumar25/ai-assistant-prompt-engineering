NovaMind AI — User Guide

Everything you need to know to use this AI Assistant and understand how prompt design shapes AI responses.

Quick Start Steps
🔑

Step 1 — Enter API Key

Paste your free Gemini API key in the banner at the top. Get one at aistudio.google.com. Your key is only stored in memory and never sent anywhere except Google's API.

🎛️

Step 2 — Pick a Function

Choose one of the 4 AI functions: Factual Q&A, Text Summarizer, Creative Writer, or Smart Advisor.

🎨

Step 3 — Choose Prompt Style

Each function offers 3 prompt styles — Simple, Detailed, or Expert — which change how the AI structures and depth of its response.

✍️

Step 4 — Type Your Input

Enter your question or text in the input box. Press Ctrl+Enter or click "Generate Response" to run it.

👍

Step 5 — Give Feedback

Rate every response as helpful (👍) or not helpful (👎). Your feedback is tracked in the session stats to compare prompt effectiveness.

📋

Step 6 — Review History

Visit the History tab anytime to review all your queries, responses, and feedback from the current session.

Functions Explained
🔍

Factual Q&A

Ask any question and receive a factual, informative answer.

  • Scientific, historical, geography questions
  • Technology and concept explanations
  • General knowledge topics
📄

Text Summarizer

Paste any long article, paragraph, or document to get a concise summary.

  • Summarize news articles or blog posts
  • Condense research papers or essays
  • Generate bullet-point overviews
✍️

Creative Writer

Generate original creative content on any theme or topic.

  • Short stories and flash fiction
  • Poems in various styles
  • Persuasive or narrative essays
💡

Smart Advisor

Receive structured advice and actionable tips on life, career, or study.

  • Study and productivity strategies
  • Career and professional advice
  • Health, wellness, and lifestyle guidance

About the Feedback Loop

After every response, you can rate it with 👍 (helpful) or 👎 (not helpful). Session totals are shown in the stats bar. Visit the History tab to see each query's feedback status and compare which prompt styles performed best.
